The models

What is scoring your file

Trained on

33 SoLEXS days

11 days of Mar 2025 and 22 days of 2026 (to 18 Oct), one-minute rows from the SDD2 light curve and PI spectrum. The last 10 days and the 7 big-flare days of 2024 were never used for training.

Nowcast

About 0.99 AUC

Works well, but a trivial rule (“a flare was on one minute ago”) scores almost the same. Labels come from a threshold detector on the same data, not an official flare list.

Forecast

Weak: about 0.6 AUC

Better than chance and than a single threshold, but about 80% of alert runs are false alarms. Treat it as a research result, not an operational warning.
